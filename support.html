Support

Questions, a bug, or a subscription problem? Email contact@dirwaas.com and a person will reply.

Where did my deleted photos go?

Everything Keepfew removes goes to Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted, where you can recover it for 30 days.

Why hasn't my storage changed yet?

iPhone keeps deleted items in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Empty that album to get the space back right away.

Cancel or manage a subscription

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Keepfew Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

Restore a purchase

In Keepfew, open Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Every photo is checked on your iPhone. Keepfew has no account and no server.
