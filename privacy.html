Keepfew Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Keepfew: Photo Cleaner ("Keepfew") is made by DIRWAAS INTELLIGENT SOLUTIONS LLC ("we").

The short version

Keepfew does not collect any data about you. Your photos and videos are examined on

your iPhone and never leave it.

Your photo library

  • Keepfew reads your photo library only after you allow it. With limited access, it reads only the photos you chose.
  • It compares photos to find duplicates, similar shots, screenshots and large videos. All of this happens on your device, using Apple's on-device frameworks.
  • The results are stored in a private file inside the app on your iPhone. It is not included in backups, and deleting Keepfew deletes it.
  • Keepfew never uploads, shares or sells your photos, videos or any information about them.
  • To show you a photo or play a video that is stored only in iCloud, your iPhone may download it from Apple's iCloud Photos, as the Photos app does. That transfer is between your device and Apple. We do not receive it.
  • Nothing is deleted unless you confirm it in the iOS prompt. Deleted items go to Photos' Recently Deleted album, where you can recover them for 30 days.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not

receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is

active.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Keepfew contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, and does not track

you across apps or websites.

Children

Keepfew is not directed to children under 13, and it collects no personal information

from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.
